## 2. Contradictions and conflicts (reported, not normalized)

1. **Currency.** The document's examples run in COP (sale COP 180M, credit in COP). SOLU keeps everything in USD before IVA (C-21). Authority limits, exposure and credit need one stated base currency. **Decision O-02.**
2. **"Margin" vs Eságara.** The document says margin / Protected Margin; SOLU's canonical word is Eságara, shown in $ and %. Screens will say Eságara.
3. **Who owns bank reconciliation and collection.** §41–42 put them in Operaciones; the module flow says Administración administers resources, the pages already live under Administración, and Module 14 has not arrived. **Decision O-06.**
4. **Hard-coded authority.** Payment limits (USD 25k) are written in code and Gerencia has every power; §7 requires a configurable, versioned matrix and that a technical administrator can't raise their own limit. **Decisions O-02, O-03.**
5. **Inventory.** The document assumes stock (physical / allocated / available, quarantine). SOLU has none, and it is not known whether the company holds stock or only passes goods through. **Decision O-01.**
6. **Internal, asset and project purchases.** Today every Supplier PO needs a Journey. Budgets and cost centres belong to Module 14. **Decision O-05.**
7. **DHL integration needs things only the company can get:** an active DHL Express account with MyDHL API access and credentials (kept in Render's environment, never in chat or code). The adapter must be written from DHL's official documentation, not from memory, and cannot be verified without those credentials. **Decision O-07.**
8. **File storage.** Receipt and dispatch photos, labels, PODs and integration evidence are stored as bytes in the database, on Neon's free plan (about 0.5 GB). This module will outgrow it. **Decision O-08.**
9. **Public surfaces.** The Showcase / SEO pages with a public Need form (§37) and "Track your solution" (§53.7) are pages for people without a login: a different kind of product surface (privacy, spam, what is exposed). **Decisions O-12, O-13.**
10. **AI and WhatsApp.** Extraction, guided capture and the official WhatsApp channel are assumed; SOLU's AI is off and WhatsApp is manual. V1 is rule-based with the AI step pluggable, as in the other modules.
11. **Events.** §52 asks for domain events with ids, causation and idempotency keys. SOLU has an audit trail and a durable job table, not an event bus. Proposal: keep the audit trail as the record and use the job table as inbox / outbox for integrations; add an explicit integration-attempt record.
12. **Roles.** §49 lists eleven functional roles; SOLU has six. Proposal: keep the six and express treasury maker / checker, receiver and logistics as permissions granted by policy, not new roles. **Decision O-04.**
13. **"SOLU expected = ETA + 3 + 1 days"** is written in code; §30 asks for the full date architecture with configurable rules.
14. **Tariff classification.** SOLU may record and compare, never choose a classification because it pays less (§12.3, AC39). No professional validation exists inside SOLU.
15. **Two selling companies.** Authority, carrier accounts and legal entity on purchases must be per company (USA / Colombia); the Colombian company's accounting gaps (pesos with IVA, withholdings) are still open from Comercial.

## 4. Build plan by waves

Each wave is committed separately, tested, and starts with its open decisions being asked.

- **O0 Handoff, Operations Home and Exceptions (§2.1, §46–48, §50; AC35, AC37) — DONE 2026-11-10.** Built: Operaciones › Inicio (`/operaciones`, `ops-home.ts`) with Needs attention (open exceptions, customs holds, disputed receipts, unconfirmed payments), Ready for next step (accepted PO without purchase, unreleased purchase, payment to verify / approve / execute, pickup pending, received not dispatched, delivered not invoiced), Customer promise at risk (Protected / Narrow / At risk / Breached / No expected date, each with its reason; carrier date never replaces the promise, `ops-rules.ts`), Waiting with control, Resource exposure in facts. `OperationalException` (kind, expected, actual, impact, evidence, options, recommended next step, owner, deadline; a bare alert is refused) with the Commercial Impact Gate (one task for the Journey owner only when promise, scope, price, compliance or relationship is touched). Operational Handoff (`/deals/[id]/handoff`), read-only and assembled from the Journey. Coverage scan now includes pickups, supplier payments, customs holds and exceptions. Policy `opsPolicy` (narrow = 2 days, upcoming = 14 days) is versioned but has no edit screen yet and its values await O-15. Not in O0: Operations Future as its own view, Profitability opportunities block (arrives with O3), inventory and FX exposure. Also closed: the "Entregado sin facturar" button no longer issues a SOLU invoice for a company that invoices through DIAN. Original scope: Operational Handoff assembled at PO acceptance from existing records (no re-typing). Operations Home with the six blocks and Operations Future, built over what exists. One `OperationalException` object (expected, actual, impact, options, owner, deadline). Commercial Impact Gate. Coverage scan extended (v1.2 §46). No new business rules needed beyond O-15: fastest value.
- **O1 Purchase Requirement (§4–6; AC1–2) — DONE 2026-11-10.** Decision O-05: customer and stock purchases now; internal, asset and project purchases wait for Module 14. Built: `PurchaseRequirement` + lines + `PurchaseAllocation` (`pr-rules.ts` `pr-v1`, `pr.ts`). A customer purchase gets its requirement automatically when the Supplier PO is prepared from the Journey (100 % assigned to it); the buyer can raise the quantity (supplier minimum) and assign units to other Journeys with an accepted PO; the draft Supplier PO quantity follows. Unassigned units = Inventory Exposure with quantity and value, authorized by Finanzas or Gerencia, never the requester (interim until the O2 matrix); the authorization covers exactly the quantity and value shown, so buying more needs a new one. Purchase Readiness is derived (purpose, product identity, quantity, supplier, offer freshness, stock authorization), each blocker with impact, way out, owner and evidence; cost above the quote's protected cost and a customer over its credit limit are shown as notes for the approver. Releasing a Supplier PO is refused while its requirement needs resolution. Operaciones › Requerimientos (list, approver queue, "+ Compra para stock"). **Limits:** a stock-only requirement can be requested and authorized but its Supplier PO arrives with inventory (O6), because Supplier POs are tied to a Journey in about 100 places; unit of measure, technical specification and expected-cost editing per line are stored but have no edit screen; the release refusal was unit-tested through the readiness rules but not clicked through in the browser (the test order had other unmet conditions). Original scope: PR with purpose, item type, economic nature; lines; allocation to one or several Deals; MOQ and unallocated quantity as Inventory Exposure; Purchase Readiness as a derived reading with blocker, impact, path, owner.
- **O2 Purchase authority (§7–11; AC3–7) — DONE 2026-11-10.** Decisions: approvers are Gerencia, Finanzas and Administración; new role `ADMINISTRACION` with the same powers as Finanzas for now (`isMoneyRole`); two levels by amount (level 1 Finanzas / Administración up to USD 25,000, Gerencia above); approval only when something is off. Built: versioned `purchasePolicy` (roles per level, limit, related-days window, triggers on/off) edited by Gerencia at Operaciones › Requerimientos; reasons STOCK, COST_ABOVE_PROTECTED, OVER_CREDIT, NEW_SUPPLIER, FULL_ADVANCE (above the limit) (`purchase-auth.ts`); Related Commitment Total = same supplier within the window for the same Journey or product, and the level is read against it (AC3); `PurchaseApproval` with the reasons, amounts, level, frozen approver context (customer sale, Eságara $ and %, credit limit, open and overdue AR, won-not-invoiced; supplier known/new, terms, open POs, advances; cash next 14 days; downside sentences), the policy version, and approve / return with question / decline / escalate to level 2; never the requester. An approval covers exactly the amount, stock and reasons it saw; anything more needs a new one. It replaces O1's separate stock decision (stock fields are still filled by it). Purchase Readiness and the Supplier PO release include it. **Limits:** amounts are compared as USD even if the requirement is in another currency; temporary delegation of approval authority (§7.4) is not built (absence coverage does not grant it); policy per company / purpose / person is not built (one policy for both companies); payment-behaviour days and FX exposure are not in the context yet; bank balances are unknown to SOLU and the screen says so; the spec's separation of system administrator and policy authority is met only as "Gerencia edits, and nobody approves their own request". Original scope: Versioned matrix per company; temporary delegation (reusing coverage); anti-fragmentation (Related Commitment Total); approval request with full context (purchase, customer credit and exposure, supplier exposure, cash, downside view); approve / ask / return / escalate.
- **O3 Buying well (§12–16; AC11–13, AC38) — DONE 2026-11-10.** Decision O-10: a saving is measured against the supplier's first quote (`SupplierPO.firstQuoteAmount` / `firstQuoteRef`, set when the order is prepared, editable while a draft; existing orders took their current total). Built on the Supplier PO page (`SpoBuying`, `buying-rules.ts`): **Antes de comprar** — negotiation window Open / Narrow / Closed from the days left before the supplier must ship, with its advice, and opportunities that carry their evidence (a cheaper last purchase of the same part, a cheaper offer from another supplier in the same currency, earlier savings with this supplier "no guarantee it repeats", other unreleased orders to consolidate, 100 % advance); no generic "ask for a discount". **Hechos de la orden** — authorized/released, sent (how, by whom), acknowledged, confirmed, as separate facts. **Confirmación del proveedor** (`SupplierConfirmation`) — recorded with its source, prefilled from the order, compared line by line: price (with economic impact), quantity, scope, ready date, payment, incoterm, currency, documents, warranty, packaging, new conditions; the functional scope (required components from the Journey's scope lines) is its own row and a lower price with a different scope is called out as not a saving (AC13); material differences need a written resolution before the order counts as confirmed; the old one-click "supplier confirmed" now leads here (AC12). **Ahorros** (`SavingsEntry`) — kind, baseline with its source, final, gross saving, passed to the customer, retained, evidence, responsible; a saving that isn't one is refused (AC38). **Limits:** the window thresholds (more than 7 days open, 2–7 narrow) are defaults in code awaiting O-15, not yet a policy screen; freight and customs / duty opportunities (§12.2–12.3, AC39) arrive with O5 and O7; the scope rows of the confirmation were unit-tested but not clicked through (the test Journey had no scope lines); a resolution is free text and does not yet create the new order version or tell Comercial by itself; there is no savings list across orders yet. Original scope: Profitability gate with negotiation window; Savings Ledger (gross, passed to customer, retained); Supplier PO facts authorized / sent / acknowledged / confirmed; Supplier Confirmation compared with the PO; functional scope confirmation.
- **O4 Paying and supplier tracking (§17–24; AC8–10, AC14) — DONE 2026-11-10.** Decision: payments follow the same approval policy as purchases. Built: payment approval authority read from the versioned `purchasePolicy` (level-1 roles up to the limit, level-2 above; never who requested or who verified the bank); the fixed limits were removed from code, so Director Comercial no longer approves payments unless Gerencia adds the role. Buy and pay stay separate decisions (purchase approval in O2, payment approval here; AC8). `PaymentExecution` records method, actual fee and effective exchange rate. Four separate facts on a payment: sent, bank debited (confirmed execution), supplier received, order released, in that order, each with how we know (`PaymentFacts`). Payment method comparison from the supplier's recorded options before paying, reusing Comercial's cost rule (extra cost, total, days to release, confirmed or not). `SupplierCommitment` with milestones by kind of order (stock 5, manufactured 7, service 4, repair 7), status reported / committed / occurred with source and last confirmation, and a reading: healthy wait before the date (no task is created, AC14), due on the date and through 1 grace day, missed afterwards (`commitment-rules.ts`). Operations home lists healthy waits under Waiting with control, due ones under Ready, missed ones under Needs attention. Bank-change hard stop and duplicate detection (AC9–10) already existed. **Limits:** maker–checker combinations by amount (§19.3, O-04) were not asked and stay as the existing rule (requester, verifier and approver are different people); the grace day is a default in code; card statement vs bank payment (AC31) and bank reconciliation arrive with O9; a missed commitment does not open an Operational Exception by itself; the method comparison and the fee / FX inputs were not clicked through in the browser (no test payment was waiting for execution with recorded options). Original scope: Buy vs pay as separate authorizations; maker–checker by policy; payment method comparison; execution record with fees, FX and the four facts; supplier commitments; adaptive milestones; waiting with control.
- **O5a Freight and shipments (§25–30; AC15, AC17, AC41) — DONE 2026-11-10 (consolidation and partial shipment still open).** Built on the Supplier PO page (`SpoFreight`, `freight-rules.ts`): `FreightRequirement` prefilled from the supply option, the order and the customer (never re-typed), each weight with who stated it; **Logistics Readiness** derived, naming each missing item and why transport can't be booked without it, with customs documents asked only for international moves; a stated weight outside what was measured before for the part asks for reconfirmation and overwrites nothing (AC15, golden step 9); `FreightQuote` options compared by **cost to fulfil**: arrival = pickup or ready date + transit + customs and last mile, read against the customer promise with its reason; choosing an option that is not the cheapest, or that doesn't protect the promise, needs a written reason. Shipment page (`ShipmentDates`): the nine separate dates of §30 (three new fields: planned departure, actual departure, expected customs release), current expected delivery as the latest of plan / carrier ETA / customs release, and promise health with its reason; the carrier date never replaces the promise (AC17). **Carrier Integration Gateway** (`carriers/gateway.ts`): adapter interface with declared capabilities and a Manual adapter; every option is typed by hand until the DHL adapter (O5b). **Not built:** shipment consolidation across several Supplier POs / Journeys and partial-shipment balance (§29, AC16) — the Shipment is still tied to one PO and one Journey; freight opportunities in the profitability gate; a Freight Requirement approval step; the clearance and last-mile days (3 + 1) remain constants in code. Original scope: Logistics readiness, Freight Requirement, option comparison, consolidation and partial shipment, full date set, promise health with its reason. Carrier Gateway with the manual adapter.

Original scope: Carrier accounts, rate, capability, create shipment, labels, pickup, tracking, pieces, POD, documents, landed cost as contrast, customer communication, tracking page. Starts only with DHL credentials (O-07) and after reading DHL's official documentation.
- **O6 Receipt, inventory and evidence (§34–35; AC21–24) — DONE 2026-11-10 (product experience and stock-only orders still open).** Decisions: goods are received in Miami and in Colombia (`StockLocation`, seeded); the Negociador Operativo, the Journey owner or management record receipts; photos stay in the database, shrunk, at most 8 per receipt, with a space meter (O-08). Each receiving exception opens an Operational Exception.
